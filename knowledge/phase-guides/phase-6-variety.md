# Phase 6: Variety

## Question
**"Can every output look unique?"**

## Goal
Prove that the system produces distinct, professional outputs every time — no two outputs look the same, yet all maintain quality standards.

## Evidence Required to Advance
- 10+ outputs that are all visually distinct
- All outputs meet professional quality bar
- Variety comes from the system, not manual tweaking
- Pipeline staleness check passes

## What to Build
- Nothing new — this phase is about PROVING the combination works
- Minor tuning of LLM prompts for more variety
- Edge case handling for unusual inputs
- Documentation of the complete system

## Patterns to Follow
- All 10 patterns should be in place by now
- Focus on P8 (Iterative Uplift) — polish without rewriting

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- All 10 anti-patterns should be absent by now
- Any remaining anti-patterns block handoff

Generate 10 videos on different topics — each has different backgrounds, transitions, typography, color palettes, hero animations, and pacing. All professional quality, all unique.
